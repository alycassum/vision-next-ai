# Wood House: furniture
Last updated: 2026-11-13
Handcrafted contemporary and classical furniture for homes, offices and hotels; custom orders welcome. Examples: living room furniture, sofas and chairs, bedroom sets, coffee and epoxy tables, acacia and shisham decor and gift pieces.

## How a custom furniture order runs
1. Preference meeting: style, material, finish, budget, and what is needed for each room.
2. Site measurement: room dimensions, door and passage widths (so the piece can be moved in), and alcoves or built-in areas.
3. References: catalog options and mood boards help narrow the style, wood type and finish.
4. Working drawings: detailed scale drawings with exact dimensions and joinery.
5. Client approval of the drawings before production, to avoid rework.
6. Quotation based on design, materials and finish.
7. Production by experienced craftsmen under Wood House supervision: frame, upholstery where applicable, any metal or glass components, and polishing or paint.
8. Quality checks mid-production and before delivery.
9. Delivery and installation, including on-site assembly for modular or built-in pieces.
10. Final walkthrough and sign-off; any touch-ups are resolved.
11. Final invoice.

## Notes
- Prices depend on design, wood, finish and size; give no figures, invite a conversation or WhatsApp enquiry for a quote.
- Timelines depend on the piece and workload; the team confirms after drawings are approved.
