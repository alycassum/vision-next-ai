Portfolio spans hotels, restaurants, corporate offices, retail and commercial spaces across Karachi and beyond. Contact: Karachi; team replies within 24 hours.

## The four firms
- Global Space: buying, selling and renting residential and commercial property, with guidance and transparency (see global-space files).
- Vision Next (design studio): tailored layouts, interior and exterior design, 3D mockups.
- Scan Line: civil work and execution based on approved designs.
- Wood House: handcrafted contemporary and classical furniture for homes, offices and hotels.

## The turn-key journey (how the firms work together)
1. Find the plot or property (Global Space): kickoff meeting, shortlisted options, visits, offer, agreement, document verification, registry.
2. Measure and plan (Vision Next): site measurement, drawings showing how much can be built under the area's bylaws.
3. Design: layouts, elevations, interior direction, 3D mockups.
4. Approvals: drawings are checked and submitted to the approving authority, usually with a few rounds of revisions.
5. Build (Scan Line): contractor selection and supervised construction through to finishing.
6. Furnish (Wood House): custom furniture made to measure.
A client can use one firm or the whole journey. Using the full journey means one team, one plan and joined-up handovers.

## Which service to suggest
- Wants to buy, sell or rent: Real Estate (Global Space).
- Has a plot or house and wants layouts, elevations, interiors or 3D views: Design Studio (Vision Next).
- Has approved drawings or a space to build or renovate: Civil Execution (Scan Line).
- Wants custom or ready furniture: Furniture (Wood House).
- Starting from scratch or wants everything handled: Full Turn-Key Solution.
