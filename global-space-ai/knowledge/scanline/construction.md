# Scan Line: civil execution
Last updated: 2026-11-13
Scan Line manages civil work based on approved designs and 3D models, keeping projects on schedule and to specification. Services: architectural construction, interior fit-out, MEP works, project management, quality control and inspection, commercial and residential builds.

## Before construction starts
- Contractor selection: two to three contractors are approached and their past work is visited and checked before they are considered. Each gives a quote against the 2D drawings with a clear breakdown (grey structure vs finishing works, and the brands and rates of materials such as steel and cement). Clients may suggest their own contractor.
- The best-fit contractor is awarded the job with agreed terms, including a fixed price (no mid-construction increases) and detailed conditions.
- Demarcation: members of the approving authority mark the exact points on site before work begins.

## Construction stages
1. Excavation to the depth required by the approved foundation layout.
2. Foundation and footings, with damp-proof course, steel checked against the structural drawing before pouring.
3. Grey structure: columns, beams and slabs floor by floor, with the supervisor checking steel binding, concrete mix and curing before moving on.
4. Brickwork or block masonry, with openings per the approved plan.
5. Electrical and plumbing rough-in, coordinated against the MEP layout before plastering.
6. Roof casting and waterproofing, with a water test.
7. Plastering inside and outside.
8. Flooring and tiling.
9. Doors and windows.
10. Electrical fixtures and testing.
11. Plumbing fixtures and testing.
12. Paint and wall finishes.
13. Kitchen and bathroom fit-out.
Throughout, the client receives periodic progress updates with site photos and progress against the agreed timeline.

## Notes
- Costs and timelines depend on size, specification and finishes; they are quoted after drawings and a contractor breakdown, never guessed in chat.
