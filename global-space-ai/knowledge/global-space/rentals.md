# Rentals (Clifton and Bath Island)
Last updated: 2026-11-13 (as of November 2026)
- 3-bedroom units: roughly 200,000 to 240,000 PKR per month.
- 4-bedroom units: roughly 250,000+ PKR per month.
- Some areas have lower rents, usually in older buildings.
- Rent depends on building age, floor, view, maintenance and parking.
- Renters should also ask about security deposit, maintenance charges, community or entrance fees, utilities, and who pays for repairs.
- Rental deals often close within a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on how realistic the asking rent is.
