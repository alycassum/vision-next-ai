# Area: Clifton (Block 7 focus, plus Blocks 2 and 5)
Last updated: 2026-11-13 (prices are as of November 2026; always say "as of November 2026" and note that live rates come from the Global Space team)

## About Global Space in Clifton
Global Space is a family-run brokerage. The family has lived in Clifton Block 7 for about 25 years, so the team knows its streets, buildings, residents and quirks closely. Block 7's layout is complicated, and local familiarity matters for identifying the right property and verifying it on the ground.

## Main focus: Block 7
- Property types: bungalows, residential plots, commercial plots, built-up residential and flats.
- Bungalows are found on Streets 7, 5, 3 and 2.
- Residential plots: roughly 300,000 to 400,000 PKR per sq yd.
- Construction cost: roughly 12,000 to 14,000 PKR per sq ft.
- Commercial plots in Clifton: roughly 2.5 to 2.7 million PKR per sq yd.
- Bungalows, 500 sq yd: roughly 23 to 28 crore PKR.
- Bungalows, 250 sq yd: roughly 8.75 to 12 crore PKR on average.
- Large flats (about 2,700 sq ft) in established Block 7 towers: roughly 11 to 13 crore PKR. Some are sold with a lease, which affects the price.

## Block 5
Older built houses and bungalow-style buildings, generally in the range of 2.5 to 4.5 crore PKR depending on size, condition and location. Good for buyers who want an established address and are open to renovating or rebuilding.

## Block 2 (near Khadijabad)
Global Space has both inventory and clients here, mainly apartments and built-up options.

## Other markets Global Space covers

## Government housing scheme
The Prime Minister's Apna Ghar loan scheme has its own eligibility conditions that buyers should read and verify themselves. Global Space has some properties that fit and others close to receiving their completion certificate that may become eligible. Never promise eligibility.

## Pricing note
Prices vary by street, condition, floor, view and urgency. Give ranges only, date-stamp them, and refer to the team for live market data.

## Common client question: where is a good place to invest?
Ask budget, purpose (living, rental income, resale) and timeline first. Then compare broad options: Block 7 plots and bungalows (land value, long-term), flats (lower entry, rental income), Block 5 older houses (lower entry, renovation upside), and new construction in Scheme 33. Never guarantee returns. Invite them to speak with the team for specific, current opportunities.
