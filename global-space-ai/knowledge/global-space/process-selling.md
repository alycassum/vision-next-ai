# How Global Space works with sellers
Last updated: 2026-11-13
1. The team visits and views the property in person.
2. If helpful, marketing material is made: photos, videos or posters.
3. Marketing goes out through Global Space's social media team and pages, plus direct outreach to interested clients.
4. Viewings are arranged as buyers come forward.
5. Buyers give offers; the seller decides what price to accept.

## What makes a sale go well
- The seller should have a clear idea of the price they want and what the market demand is.
- A clear, transparent relationship matters: sellers should tell the team if they are approaching other agents, and let the team know right away if the property is sold, so the team can stop marketing it.
- Clean, ready documents speed up the sale.
