# How Global Space works with buyers (plots and properties)
Last updated: 2026-11-13
1. Kickoff meeting: understand budget, preferences, preferred area, timeline and plot size.
2. Search: options matching the budget and preferences are researched, with exact locations, quoted demand and room for negotiation. This uses the team's database, calls to other brokers, and confirming current availability with owners. Typically about a week.
3. Options sheet: shortlisted options are compiled in an organised, dated sheet so the client knows when the market information was collected.
4. Visits: the client is taken to view the options, with honest pros and cons of each location, foot traffic, future prospects (capital appreciation, planned infrastructure) and whether the plot can practically be built on for the client's needs.
5. Follow-up and offer: the client decides on an offer, which is passed to the seller; the team negotiates both sides toward a fair price.
6. Closing: the deal is finalised by message or email, or in a meeting when the gap is small. Payment terms are agreed at this stage.
7. Agreement: a written agreement is drafted with full details of the property, seller, buyer and agreed price, with both parties' CNIC details. It is shared for review and revisions, then printed on stamp paper.
8. Token and signing: a meeting with buyer, seller and witnesses to sign and hand over the token. The deal is clearly conditional on the documents being valid and fully verified.
9. Documents: the seller's documents are collected and checked, including NOC, mutation, owner's name and chain of ownership, and any gift deed. A lawyer verifies them and prepares the registry.
10. Registry: the team follows up with the lawyer and registry staff, reviews the sale deed for errors, and government taxes, duties and challans are paid before registry. Everything is done through proper, documented channels. The date is coordinated with all parties.
11. After registry: the buyer keeps the original sale deed, copies go to the seller and the records.

If documents have problems, the deal is paused: issues are fixed and the deal resumes, or it becomes invalid under the verification condition. Timelines vary from days to several months, mostly depending on price realism and how quickly documents are cleared.

## Tips for buyers
- Be clear on budget and must-haves before viewing.
- Make offers based on comparable recent activity, not asking prices alone.
- See buyer-checklist.md.
- For taxes and legal points, rely on a qualified lawyer or tax advisor; the team coordinates with them.
