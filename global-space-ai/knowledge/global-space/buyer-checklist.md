# What a strong buyer or renter should ask and check
Last updated: 2026-11-13

## Questions to ask about any property
1. Exact covered area (sq ft) and plot size (sq yd), and whether they match the documents.
2. Exact location and street, and how it fits the buyer's needs.
3. Structural integrity of the house, building or flat; recommend an independent structural check where relevant.
4. Utilities: electricity connection (including meter and load), water supply, gas, and any issues with each.
5. Security: gated community, guards, building access.
6. Documents: any issues or disputes with the paperwork; completion certificate status where applicable.
7. Hidden or extra charges: community fees, entrance or joining fees, transfer fees, and similar charges on buying or renting in gated communities or apartment buildings.
8. Building maintenance: monthly charges, condition of lifts, common areas, generator and water backup, and who manages upkeep.
9. For leased properties (for example flats sold with lease): remaining lease terms and transfer conditions.

## Reminder for the consultant
Give this as a practical checklist. Legal verification and tax questions go to a qualified lawyer or tax advisor; the Global Space team can help coordinate.
